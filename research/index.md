My work spans computational mass spectrometry, mechanistic modeling, and research infrastructure. A recurring aim is to represent scientific assumptions explicitly in software, so that workflows preserve useful structure and are easier to test and reason about.

## Current threads

- **Computational mass spectrometry:** LC-HRMS/MS feature extraction, MS2 annotation, fragment alignment, spectral redundancy reduction, and consensus spectra.
- **Modeling and automation:** BacArena-based simulation and closed-loop experimental workflows.
- **Research software:** Graph and topology methods, uncertainty-aware representations, and documentation-driven workflows.
